Reporting

Weekly report

Each week, sales staff will submit a short activity report. The live form is not built yet. It will later be embedded on this page and will write responses to a Google Sheet for the Regional Head and KPI tracking.

What the form is for

The weekly report is a simple check-in, not a payroll calculation. Monthly KPI and salary conditions are still calculated on the 1st of the month (with a mid-month progress check around the 15th) as described on the Pay, KPI & Commission page. The weekly form helps managers see pipeline, new clients, IB progress, and blockers in time to help.

  • Submitted once per week by each salesperson.
  • Responses will be stored in a Google Sheet (to be connected).
  • Do not put salary amounts, personal ID documents, or client passwords in the form.
  • Client names or account references should follow whatever sharing standard the Regional Head sets when the form goes live.

Risk warning

Trading contracts for difference (CFDs) and forex involves a high risk of losing money, and may not be suitable for every client. Markets can move quickly. There is no guarantee of profit, and past activity is not a reliable indicator of future results. Nothing on this internal site is an offer, a recommendation, or a promise of returns.
