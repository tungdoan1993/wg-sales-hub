Questions

Frequently asked questions

Short answers drafted from the salary and KPI conditions that apply from 1 December 2026, and from the current operating rules used in this build. November salary is not calculated under the new conditions. Deposits generated in November count toward December's calculation. Sales commission is 3% of Net from 1 November 2026 (November included); commission eligibility conditions and KPI rules are unchanged. Where something is still open, the answer says so. This is not a substitute for a Regional Head or HR decision.

Eligibility and timing

When do the new salary and KPI conditions start?

From 1 December 2026, until further update. November salary is not calculated under the new conditions. Deposits generated in November count toward December's calculation. The first two months from your join date remain a training / probation period (học việc) with no sales targets. The sales commission rate of 3% of Net is a separate change and applies from 1 November 2026 (November included); commission eligibility conditions and KPI rules are unchanged.

When do sales targets start after I join?

The first two months from your join date are a training / probation period (học việc). No sales targets apply in those months: not the USD 500 new-client deposit salary condition, and not the commission KPI. Both start from the 3rd month. Example (labelled example only): if you join on 10 March, March and April are học việc; May is your first month with both the salary condition and the commission KPI.

Do I still need to meet a condition to receive salary?

Yes, but only from month 3. Under the current mechanism, from month 3 onward you receive salary for a month when your NEW clients deposit at least USD 500 in total in that month. A client is new by first-deposit date, and the USD 500 total includes all deposits those new clients make in the month — not only the first deposit. New retail clients and new clients brought through your IBs both count. Existing (old) clients do not count. Example (labelled example only): clients A, B, C, D, and E each make a first deposit of USD 100 that month; the total is USD 500 and the salary condition is met because the deposit total is USD 500, not because there are five people. Registration count is not a salary condition. If the salary condition is not met, the salesperson is not dismissed; they move to freelance status. This page never states salary amounts. The first two months from your join date are học việc: this USD 500 salary condition does not apply then.

I reached USD 500 but have fewer registrations. Is the salary condition met?

Yes. The salary condition is met when total deposits in the month from new clients reach at least USD 500. The number of registrations does not matter, and there is no “5 registrations” condition.

I have 5 or more registrations but only USD 400 in deposits. Is the salary condition met?

No. The salary condition is not met. Only the USD 500 total of deposits from new clients in the month counts. Registration count is not a salary condition.

What if I do not meet the USD 500 salary condition?

The salesperson is not dismissed. They move to freelance status.

How is a “new client” decided?

Confirmed: a new client is counted by first-deposit date. For the USD 500 salary condition, all deposits that new client makes in the same month count, not only the first deposit. Existing (old) clients do not count toward that total.

Are salary-hold tiers used now?

No. The salary-hold tiers in the original WeGolden KPI Handbook are not applied to sales staff for now. There is no salary-hold level in the current mechanism. The original handbook remains the target model for a later stage, once the business is stable and revenue is large.

Commission and KPI paths

When is commission paid?

From month 3 onward, commission for a month is paid only if you meet one of the two KPIs: Path A (at least one new qualified IB in the month) or Path B (Net Level 1 of at least USD 3,000 and at least two new retail clients in the month, each full-verified, with a deposit of at least USD 300 and trading of at least 0.5 lot). Meeting either path is enough. The first two months from your join date are học việc: no commission KPI applies then.

What makes an IB a “qualified IB”?

A qualified IB has at least two full-verified clients, each with a deposit of at least USD 300 and trading of at least 0.5 lot, and a total IB deposit of at least USD 2,000. The IB counts in the month it qualifies, not the month the profile was registered. A short explainer with examples is on the Qualified IB page.

What is Net Level 1?

Net = Deposit − Withdrawal − Commission. Level 1 means clients directly under you, not through an MIB or IB. Path B (eligibility) requires Net L1 of at least USD 3,000 in the month, plus at least two qualifying new retail clients. This is not the same figure as the 3% commission base, which uses Net of the entire downline.

Has the commission rate changed?

Yes. Sales commission is 3% of Net from 1 November 2026 (November included). Commission conditions and KPI rules are unchanged. The salary and KPI conditions (including the USD 500 salary condition and freelance status) still apply from 1 December 2026; November salary is not calculated under those conditions.

What is the sales commission rate?

Confirmed: from 1 November 2026 (November included), sales staff earn 3% of Net (Deposit − Withdrawal − Commission) of the salesperson’s entire downline — Sale → MIB → IB → clients — including direct clients and all clients under MIBs and IBs. Commission is paid only if a KPI path is met. Path B eligibility still uses Net Level 1 (direct clients only), as in the document; that test is separate from the 3% payout base. Worked examples on the Pay, KPI & Commission page use invented figures only.

What about sales-leader commission?

The stated leader rates are 2.5% of the net the leader develops themselves, plus 10% of their team members’ commission. Leader rules are not applied yet and are marked Coming soon. They will be confirmed separately.

Tracking and exceptions

When are monthly results calculated?

On the 1st of each month, by WG KPI Audit (AI assistant), from the WeGolden admin system, excluding test accounts. The Regional Head reviews the results before HR applies them to payroll. A mid-month progress check is done around the 15th.

Can a team have a longer onboarding window?

The source document notes that the Regional Head may instruct an additional period to find clients, with the start month still to be confirmed. This draft does not apply any unofficial exception. Ask the Regional Head before assuming a different KPI start month.

Does this site show my salary or anyone else’s pay?

No. This internal hub explains rules and uses labelled example figures only. It must never show actual salary amounts or any individual’s pay or financial data.

Risk warning

Trading contracts for difference (CFDs) and forex involves a high risk of losing money, and may not be suitable for every client. Markets can move quickly. There is no guarantee of profit, and past activity is not a reliable indicator of future results. Nothing on this internal site is an offer, a recommendation, or a promise of returns.
