How to use this draft

Read Pay, KPI & Commission first. Worked examples use invented, labelled figures only — they are not anyone’s actual salary, commission, or client book. Points that still need a decision are flagged on that page and in the FAQ.

Scope of this version

This site is an internal English draft. Arabic and Farsi versions are planned; the layout uses logical spacing so right-to-left pages can be added later. The language switcher in the header is not active yet.

Risk warning

Trading contracts for difference (CFDs) and forex involves a high risk of losing money, and may not be suitable for every client. Markets can move quickly. There is no guarantee of profit, and past activity is not a reliable indicator of future results. Nothing on this internal site is an offer, a recommendation, or a promise of returns.
