Pay · KPI · Commission

Pay, KPI and commission, explained simply

This page explains when salary is paid, when commission is earned, and how the rates work. All numeric walk-throughs are labelled as examples and use invented figures only. This page never shows a real person’s salary or pay.

Source: WeGolden MENA Sales KPI & Commission Mechanism, effective 17 November 2026, until further update. Current operating notes used in this draft are listed where they add to, or may differ from, the document.

1. Context

WeGolden is a new brand in the market, so the current mechanism is adjusted to present conditions. The original WeGolden KPI Handbook (including salary-hold tiers) remains the target model once the business is stable and revenue is large. That target model is not applied to sales staff for now.

2. Training / probation period (học việc)

The first two months from each person’s join date are a training / probation period (học việc). No sales targets apply in those months (confirmed):

  • The USD 500 new-client deposit salary condition does not apply.
  • The commission KPI (Path A or Path B) does not apply.
  • Both the salary condition and the commission KPI start from the 3rd month after joining.
Example figures only — not a real employee

Example: when targets start

Alex joins on 10 March 2026. Month 1 is March and month 2 is April. These two months are học việc — no salary-condition target and no commission KPI. Alex’s first month with both targets is May 2026 (month 3).

Confirmed

Months 1–2 from the join date have no sales targets. The USD 500 salary condition and the commission KPI both begin in month 3.

3. Salary condition

From month 3 onward, a sales staff member receives salary for a month when their NEW clients deposit at least USD 500 in total in that month. This condition does not apply during the first two months (học việc).

  • A client is “new” by first-deposit date (confirmed).
  • The USD 500 threshold counts all deposits made in the month by those new clients, not only the first deposit (confirmed).
  • New retail clients count.
  • New clients brought through the salesperson’s IBs also count.
  • Existing (old) clients do not count toward this USD 500 total.
  • The salary-hold tiers in the original handbook (20% / 30% / 50%) are not applied to sales staff for now. There is no salary-hold level in the current mechanism.
Confirmed

A new client is counted by first-deposit date. For the salary condition, add up every deposit those new clients make in the same month. Reaching USD 500 meets the salary condition. This is not the commission KPI.

Example A — five new clients, first deposit USD 100 each

In June, five clients (A, B, C, D, and E — example names only) each make a first deposit of USD 100. Their first-deposit date falls in June, so all five are new clients for the month.

Client A: USD 100
Client B: USD 100
Client C: USD 100
Client D: USD 100
Client E: USD 100
Total deposits from new clients in June: 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 = USD 500

USD 500 meets the threshold → salary condition for June is met. (This does not state or imply any salary amount.)

Example B — later deposits in the same month also count

In June, three clients have their first deposit in June. Client B deposits again later in the month. All of those June deposits count:

Retail client A: first deposit USD 200
Retail client B: first deposit USD 150, then a further USD 50 in June (total from B in June: USD 200)
Client C, introduced via an IB: first deposit USD 150
Total deposits from new clients in June: 200 + 200 + 150 = USD 550

USD 550 is at least USD 500 → salary condition for June is met. (This does not state or imply any salary amount.)

Example C — salary condition not met

In July, new-client deposits total USD 400. An existing client also deposits USD 2,000 in July. The existing-client deposit does not count.

New-client deposits: USD 400. Old-client deposits: ignored. Total that counts: USD 400.

USD 400 is below USD 500 → salary condition for July is not met.

4. Commission condition (KPI)

From month 3 onward, commission for a month is paid only when the salesperson meets one of the two KPIs below. Meeting either path is enough. During the training / probation period (học việc), months 1–2, no commission KPI applies.

Path A — Standard IB KPI

At least 1 new qualified IB in the month.

A qualified IB has all of the following:

  • At least 2 full-verified clients, each with a deposit of at least USD 300 and trading of at least 0.5 lot, and
  • A total IB deposit of at least USD 2,000.

The IB counts in the month it qualifies, not the month the profile was registered. A standalone explainer is on the Qualified IB page.

Path B — Retail client KPI

Both of the following in the month:

  • Net Level 1 of at least USD 3,000, and
  • At least 2 new retail clients, each full-verified, with a deposit of at least USD 300 and trading of at least 0.5 lot.

Net = Deposit − Withdrawal − Commission.

Level 1 means clients directly under the salesperson, not through an IB.

Example figures only — invented IB, not a real partner

Example C — Path A, qualified IB (counts in the qualification month)

IB “Northwind Partners” (example name) registers a profile in February. In March the IB has two full-verified clients:

Client 1: deposit USD 800, traded 0.8 lot — meets the USD 300 and 0.5 lot tests
Client 2: deposit USD 1,400, traded 0.5 lot — meets the tests
Total IB deposit: 800 + 1,400 = USD 2,200 (at least USD 2,000)

The IB qualifies in March, so it counts for March Path A — not for February, even though the profile was registered then. Path A is met for March.

Example figures only — invented activity, not a real book

Example D — Path A, not yet qualified

An IB has two full-verified clients with deposits of USD 300 and USD 400, each traded 0.5 lot. Total IB deposit is USD 700.

The per-client tests pass, but total IB deposit is below USD 2,000. The IB is not yet qualified, so Path A is not met on this IB alone.

Example figures only — invented activity, not a real book

Example E — Path B, retail KPI met

In June, a salesperson has two new retail clients, both full-verified, each depositing USD 400 and trading 0.6 lot. Across Level 1 (direct clients, not through an IB):

Level 1 deposits: USD 4,200
Level 1 withdrawals: USD 800
Commission (as used in the Net formula): USD 200
Net Level 1 = 4,200 − 800 − 200 = USD 3,200

Net L1 is at least USD 3,000, and there are at least two qualifying new retail clients → Path B is met for June.

Example figures only — invented activity, not a real book

Example F — Path B, not met (Net L1 too low)

Two qualifying new retail clients are in place, but Net Level 1 is USD 2,400.

The headcount test passes, but Net L1 is below USD 3,000 → Path B is not met. Commission would still be payable if Path A (a qualified IB) is met in the same month; otherwise it is not.

5. Commission rates

  • Sales staff: 2% of Net of the salesperson’s entire downline (confirmed). Net = Deposit − Withdrawal − Commission.
  • The entire downline is Sale → MIB → IB → clients. That includes the salesperson’s direct clients and all clients under their MIBs and IBs.
  • This payout base is not the same figure used for Path B. Path B eligibility still uses Net Level 1 only (direct clients, not through an MIB or IB), as in the document.
  • Sales leader: 2.5% of the net they develop themselves, plus 10% of their team members’ commission. Coming soon

Leader rules are not applied yet. They will be confirmed separately. Do not use the leader rates for current payroll until that confirmation is issued.

Commission at 2% is calculated on Net of this entire downline. Path B eligibility still uses Net Level 1 only — direct clients under the Sale, not through an MIB or IB.

Two different “Net” figures

Path B (to become eligible): Net Level 1 — direct clients only, at least USD 3,000, plus at least two qualifying new retail clients. Commission payout (once eligible via Path A or Path B): 2% of Net across the entire downline, including clients under MIBs and IBs.

Example figures only — not anyone’s actual commission or book

Example G — 2% on entire-downline Net, if eligible

Assume the salesperson has already met Path A or Path B for the month. Invented activity for the month:

Direct clients (Level 1): deposits USD 4,000 − withdrawals USD 500 − commission USD 100 = Net L1 USD 3,400
Clients under MIBs and IBs: deposits USD 6,000 − withdrawals USD 800 − commission USD 400 = Net USD 4,800
Entire downline Net = 3,400 + 4,800 = USD 8,200
Sales commission = 2% × 8,200 = USD 164

Path B, if it were being tested, would look only at Net L1 (USD 3,400 in this example) plus the two new retail-client tests — not at the USD 8,200. The 2% payout uses the entire downline Net (USD 8,200), not Net L1 alone.

If the same person is not eligible under either KPI path, commission for that month is not paid, regardless of downline Net.

6. How results are tracked

  • Monthly KPI results are calculated on the 1st of each month by WG KPI Audit (AI assistant) from the WeGolden admin system.
  • Test accounts are excluded.
  • The Regional Head reviews the results before HR applies them to payroll.
  • A mid-month progress check is done around the 15th.

7. Open points and possible conflicts

Where the source document and the current operating rules used for this build are not fully aligned, or a rule is incomplete, both are kept and flagged rather than silently merged.

Flagged for review
  • Leader rules. Rates are stated (2.5% of self-developed net + 10% of team commission) but are not applied yet — marked Coming soon.
  • Team-specific timing. The document notes that some onboarding windows (for example an additional period to find clients) may be set by the Regional Head, with the start month still to be confirmed. This site does not apply any unofficial exception.
Aligned between the document and current rules

The first two months from the join date are a training / probation period (học việc) with no sales targets: neither the USD 500 salary condition nor the commission KPI applies; both start in month 3. A new client is counted by first-deposit date, and the USD 500 salary condition uses all deposits those new clients make in the month. Commission eligibility is either at least one qualified IB, or Net L1 of at least USD 3,000 plus at least two new retail clients. Path B still uses Net Level 1 (direct clients only). Once eligible, sales commission is 2% of Net of the entire downline (Sale → MIB → IB → clients). Salary-hold tiers are not in use. The document adds the qualification tests (full verification, USD 300, 0.5 lot, IB total USD 2,000) and the rule that an IB counts in the month it qualifies.
