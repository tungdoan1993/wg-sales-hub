KPI · Path A

Qualified IB

This page explains when an Introducing Broker is a qualified IB for a salesperson’s monthly commission KPI (Path A). The rules below come only from the WeGolden MENA Sales KPI & Commission Mechanism, effective 17 November 2026, until further update.

When Path A is met

Commission for a month is paid only if the salesperson meets one of two KPIs. Path A is: at least 1 new qualified IB in the month. (Path B — Net Level 1 and new retail clients — is explained on the Pay, KPI & Commission page.)

What makes an IB qualified

A qualified IB has all of the following:

  • At least 2 full-verified clients, each with a deposit of at least USD 300 and trading of at least 0.5 lot, and
  • A total IB deposit of at least USD 2,000.

The IB counts in the month it qualifies, not the month the profile was registered.

Example — qualifies in March, not in the registration month

IB “Northwind Partners” (example name) registers a profile in February. In March the IB has two full-verified clients:

Client 1: deposit USD 800, traded 0.8 lot — meets the USD 300 and 0.5 lot tests
Client 2: deposit USD 1,400, traded 0.5 lot — meets the tests
Total IB deposit: 800 + 1,400 = USD 2,200 (at least USD 2,000)

The IB qualifies in March, so it counts for March Path A — not for February. Path A is met for March.

Example — not yet qualified

An IB has two full-verified clients with deposits of USD 300 and USD 400, each traded 0.5 lot. Total IB deposit is USD 700.

The per-client tests pass, but total IB deposit is below USD 2,000. The IB is not yet qualified, so Path A is not met on this IB alone.

How this sits with commission payout

Qualifying an IB is the Path A eligibility test. Once the salesperson is eligible (via Path A or Path B), sales commission is 2% of Net of the entire downline (Sale → MIB → IB → clients), not only the clients under that one IB. Path B eligibility still uses Net Level 1 (direct clients only). See Commission rates.
